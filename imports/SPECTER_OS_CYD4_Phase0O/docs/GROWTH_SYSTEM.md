# SPECTER//OS Growth System

## Design rule
Growth changes Ghost's identity and expressive tendencies without making the core care loop harder.

Needs:
SIGNAL / INTEGRITY / MOOD / CHARGE

remain separate from long-term growth.

## Long-term axes
CURIOUS
DATA-oriented, more scan behavior.

PLAYFUL
Interaction-oriented, more chirps.

STEADY
Integrity-oriented, calmer observe bias and reduced glitch tendency.

HARMONIC
Energy/balance-oriented, stronger observe bias.

BALANCED
No axis dominates by a meaningful margin.

## Safety / gating
Personality does not bypass station thresholds and does not choose functional destinations during ordinary patrol.
